How FreelanceHub Works

FreelanceHub is an online marketplace where clients post projects and skilled freelancers send proposals to win the work. It's simple, fast and free to get started.

1

Post or Find a Project

Clients describe what they need, and freelancers browse open projects that match their skills.

2

Send and Review Proposals

Freelancers pitch their ideas and budget, and clients compare the proposals they receive.

3

Work Together

Pick the best match, agree on the deadline, and track the project from your dashboard.
